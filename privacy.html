Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026. The Brief Reader is published by Seth Paxton. For privacy questions, contact sethpaxton@gmail.com.

How the app handles information

The Brief has no developer-operated account or server, advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking SDK. Seth Paxton does not receive your reading library, calendar, saved city, Unread tasks, or summary prompts from the app.

Your subscriptions, downloaded articles and images, summaries, reading position, saved/read/pinned state, and preferences are stored on your device. The Safari extension uses a shared Keychain group to pass feed subscriptions to the app on that device.

Private iCloud sync

When iCloud is available, subscriptions, feed names, categories, and subscription-change information sync using your private CloudKit database through Apple. Downloaded articles, summaries, and reading progress are not included in this feed sync. The publisher cannot read your private CloudKit records. Apple’s services are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Optional sources

Calendar access is requested only when you enable it. The app reads your selected calendars to show today’s agenda and upcoming events. It does not edit calendars; displayed event information stays in memory and is not sent to the publisher.

Weather uses a city you select, not device location permission. City search uses Apple MapKit, and the selected place’s coordinates are sent to Apple WeatherKit to retrieve forecasts. Your city selection and a short-lived forecast cache are stored locally. Disabling weather clears its forecast cache.

If you enable Unread integration, The Brief reads the task/focus snapshot Unread shares with its widgets through an app group on the same device. The Brief does not edit those tasks or send them to the publisher. Disable this source to stop displaying it.

Summaries and publisher websites

AI summaries use Apple’s on-device Foundation Models framework. Article text is not sent to an external AI service by The Brief. Summaries may contain errors.

The app contacts the feed, article, and image hosts you choose directly. Those hosts receive network requests, including your IP address and requested URLs, and may keep logs under their own policies. Opening external links in Safari also follows the destination’s policies. The Brief does not control publisher websites or their data practices.

Your choices and retention

You may revoke calendar permission in system Settings and disable optional sources within the app. Remove unwanted subscriptions in Settings; saved and pinned articles remain after feed removal. Ordinary article cleanup follows your selected retention period (five days by default), while saved and pinned stories are retained. Uninstalling the app removes its local app container; iCloud records and shared Keychain data can persist separately. Manage iCloud app data in Apple’s account/storage settings. Contact us for help understanding your options.

Support and this website

If you email support, the email address and information you choose to provide are used to respond and troubleshoot, and retained as needed to handle the request or legal obligations. You can request deletion by email, subject to such obligations. Do not include sensitive calendar or account information.

This support site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no added analytics or advertising scripts. GitHub may process technical request information to host and secure the site under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children and changes

The Brief is a general-audience reader and is not directed at children under 13. Feeds and linked websites may contain content unsuitable for children. We may update this policy when app behavior changes; the effective date above identifies the current policy.
