The Brief Reader

Your publications, in a quiet daily edition. The Brief is an RSS and Atom reader for iPhone and iPad, with saved articles, on-demand summaries, and optional calendar, weather, and Unread focus.

Get started

Tap + to add a publication’s website or RSS/Atom feed. Tap a headline to read. Swipe to save or mark a story read, or press and hold to pin it. In Settings, choose how long to keep downloaded stories. Saved and pinned stories are kept through normal cleanup.

Optional features

Subscriptions, feed names, and categories sync through your private iCloud account when available. Downloaded stories and reading progress stay on each device. AI summaries require a compatible device with Apple Intelligence enabled and its on-device model ready. Generate them by expanding “AI summary”; cached summaries can be read offline.

Open Your Brief to opt into calendar access, choose a weather city, or display the focus shared by Unread on the same device. You can use the reader without these sources. Weather needs an internet connection. The Safari share extension adds feed subscriptions.

Troubleshooting

If a feed fails, confirm its address and try again with an internet connection. Some sites do not offer feeds or readable full text; The Brief does not bypass paywalls. For iCloud issues, check your iCloud sign-in and the sync status in Settings. Cached content remains available when a refresh fails.
